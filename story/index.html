Page 1 — Define the KPI

The 75.8% rate uses only batches aged 3 years or more (Summer 2023 and earlier). Include Fall 2026 and the rate goes up because those companies have not had time to be marked Inactive.

The public Y Combinator directory lists 6,378 launched companies. 4,407 are Active, 840 Acquired, 23 Public, and 1,108 Inactive. A rate taken over that whole list is not a performance metric. Fall 2026 has not had time to fail.

KPI. Still-operating rate, aged 3 years. Count of companies whose status is Active, Acquired, or Public, divided by companies whose batch is at least 3 years old as of 2026-11-09. Inactive is the only closed label. Acquired and Public stay in the numerator: they are exits, not failures. The 3-year floor is the logic. Without it, this year's batch inflates the rate.

On that definition, 3,275 of 4,322 companies from Summer 2023 and earlier are still operating. The rate is 75.8%.

The directory filled with something else. AI tags were 21.8% of the 2021 batches and 64.1% of 2024. Consumer was 19.1% of 2018 and 5.5% of 2026. Winter and Spring 2026 are missing tags on most records, so the 2026 AI share is not a finding.

Age confounds a naive AI comparison. AI companies are younger, so they look more alive. Restrict to the 1,808 companies from 2018–2021, which have had five to eight years. Consumer inactive rate 40.1% (101 of 252). Fintech 16.5%. Healthcare 15.3%. B2B 21.0%. That is the mechanism: YC moved away from the industry that dies on this file.

Action. Score a YC-style portfolio on the 3-year still-operating rate by industry, not on this year's headcount of AI tags. Do not copy consumer marketplaces from the famous public companies (Airbnb, Instacart, DoorDash sit in Consumer and they are the exceptions). The first cut is Consumer versus Fintech and Healthcare in any cohort that is old enough to measure.

This is YC's launched directory, not startups in general, not jobs, and not round-by-round funding. Status is a YC label. Team size is a current snapshot.

Page 4 — Decision

If the next batch is scored on how many companies it produced, or on how many carry an AI tag, the score will always look like recent YC. That is volume, not the KPI.

If it is scored on the 3-year still-operating rate by industry, Consumer is the group to stop copying from the public-company poster. Fintech and Healthcare are the groups that remain operating on this file.

Do not read the 2024–2026 inactive rates as a win for AI. Those cohorts are not yet in the denominator.

What this is not

Not a startup census
Launched YC companies with a public directory page. Stealth and unlisted companies are missing.
Not jobs
No employment. Team size is a current snapshot, often empty or rounded.
Not funding
No round amounts. YC’s check is a standard deal. Variation is who they accept.
Not a failure rate for startups
Inactive is YC’s label. Companies that never entered the directory cannot die in it.
